# Vision & Positioning

## Vision Statement

> **Become the definitive authority platform for professional cleaning B2B in France — the first source decision-makers, AI engines, and journalists consult when they need data, guidance, or tools about the cleaning industry.**

## Mission

Equip French cleaning company owners and decision-makers with the information, tools, and data they need to professionalize, digitize, and grow their businesses — while building the most comprehensive structured database of the French cleaning industry.

## Strategic Positioning

### What CleanP Is

- A **B2B media platform** specialized 100% in professional cleaning in France
- A **structured database** of cleaning companies, prices, regulations, and market data
- A **comparison and evaluation tool** for software and services
- A **free utility provider** (calculators, templates, generators)
- A **citation-worthy source** for AI engines (GEO-optimized)

### What CleanP Is NOT

- A consumer cleaning guide (no "how to clean my bathroom")
- A generic business blog (no "10 tips to motivate your team")
- An AI-generated content farm (no slop, no thin content, no mass production)
- A pure lead-generation front (the content must have standalone value)
- A software vendor (Proprely sells software; CleanP informs and compares independently)

### Positioning Statement (Fill-in-the-blank)

**For** owners and managers of professional cleaning companies (TPE/PME) in France
**who** struggle to find reliable, specialized information about their industry,
**CleanP** is the authoritative B2B media platform
**that** combines expert editorial content, structured industry data, free tools, and software comparisons
**unlike** generic business media or software vendor blogs,
**because** we are 100% focused on the cleaning industry and produce independent, data-driven, expert content.

## Unique Value Proposition (UVP)

**"L'information, les données et les outils dont les pros de la propreté ont besoin — tout en un."**

**English:** "The information, data, and tools that cleaning pros need — all in one place."

## Differentiators vs. Competition

| Competitor Type | Their Weakness | Our Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Software vendor blogs (Propret, 2BePragma) | Product-focused, no editorial independence, limited SEO | Independent media + comparison + directory |
| Generic directories (Annuaire-du-nettoyage) | No content, no enrichment, poor UX, no data | Editorial + data-rich profiles + local content |
| Industry federations (FNTP) | Institutional tone, rare publishing, no SEO | Modern UX, frequent publishing, GEO-optimized |
| General business media (BPI, Xerfi) | Horizontal, no cleaning specialization | Vertical 100%, deep expertise |
| Horizontal SaaS comparators | No cleaning-specific criteria | Specialized comparison grid, expert criteria |

## Brand Voice & Tone

| Dimension | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Tone** | Professional but accessible. Expert without being academic. |
| **Person** | Third-person editorial for articles; second-person direct for tools/CTAs |
| **Style** | Data-backed every claim. No fluff. No "revolutionary" or "game-changer". |
| **Expertise** | Demonstrate deep sector knowledge (NAF codes, regulation articles, real prices) |
| **Neutrality** | CleanP is independent. When mentioning Proprely, it's disclosed and justified. |
| **Action-oriented** | Every article should leave the reader with a concrete next step or tool |

## Content Authority Pillars

The platform's authority rests on 5 pillars — every piece of content must align with at least one:

1. **Regulatory expertise** — IDCC 3043, labor law, safety standards, updates
2. **Market data** — Statistics, benchmarks, studies, original surveys
3. **Software intelligence** — Independent comparison of cleaning management tools
4. **Operational know-how** — Management, productivity, quality control, HR
5. **Local market knowledge** — City-by-city, region-by-region data and analysis

## Competitive Moat (Defensibility)

1. **Data moat** — Structured database of 15,500+ companies enriched over time. Hard to replicate.
2. **Content freshness moat** — Continuous regulatory updates require ongoing editorial work.
3. **GEO moat** — First-mover advantage in AI citations for the sector. 3-5 year window.
4. **Tool moat** — Free calculators and templates create habit and return visits.
5. **Network moat** — Directory + Proprely + Réseau Propreté = ecosystem lock-in.
