## Why This Project Exists

The French professional cleaning market is a **EUR 21 billion industry** with **15,500+ companies** employing 600,000+ people. It is extremely fragmented (90%+ TPE/PME), digitally immature, and underserved by specialized B2B media. No single platform combines editorial authority, structured data, free tools, and software comparison for this sector.

CleanP fills this gap by becoming the **definitive authority platform** for professional cleaning B2B in France — capturing qualified organic traffic through SEO and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), then converting it into leads for Proprely (SaaS) and Réseau Propreté (marketplace/directory).

## Guiding Principles

1. **MVP is lean** — Build only what captures traffic and converts it. No automation V1.
2. **GEO-first content** — Every piece of content must be citable by AI engines.
3. **Data-driven authority** — Statistics, studies, benchmarks > opinions.
4. **No AI slop** — AI can assist, never replace editorial judgment.
5. **Scalable architecture** — Database schema and code must support V2 automation without migration.
6. **French market only** — All content, data, legal compliance = France.
7. **B2B professional tone** — Credible, expert, never consumer-oriented.
